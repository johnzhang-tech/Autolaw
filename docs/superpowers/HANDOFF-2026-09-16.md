## What the job is

The home page used to explain the product as a seven-chapter pinned-scroll story ("Chapter 1: 214 documents, out of order"). The owner judged it too technical for lawyers. It has been replaced by a Product section naming features in the practice's own words, split into **Pre-Litigation** and **Litigation**.

**The current instruction (2026-09-16):** follow anytimeai.ai. A **Product dropdown** in the nav, two phase columns of feature links, and **one page per feature at `/product/<slug>/`** — 13 pages. Expandable cards on the home page are rejected; the home Product section becomes the same two columns as linked cards. The landing page is a sales asset and should read like one: outcome-led, bold, exciting — not a spec sheet.

## Constraints that are not negotiable

The previous version of this site claimed things the product does not do, and a correction release exists because of it. `src/__tests__/claims.test.tsx` fails the build on retired phrases — including `certified`, `generate documents`, records-request drafting, "separate products", "intake calls".

- **`Discovery Responses` and `Deposition Review` do not exist in the product.** They may appear — the owner wants the full vision on the page — but must carry a forward-looking label, never read as available today. No page for either.
- **Accident & Injury Illustrations is `early-access`:** the engine (`/Users/zhongzhang/projects/media-engine`, ~19k LOC, 1,563 tests) is real, but `MEDIA_ENGINE_ENABLED` defaults off and the case-app screen is behind a work-in-progress toggle. It gets a page; the page says it runs on your case with our team during a pilot.
- **Never publish an accuracy percentage for the illustrations**, and never "medically validated", "expert-reviewed", "court-admissible", "clinician-approved", "3D", or surgical-procedure claims. Its own accuracy corpus is directional and the gating test is literally named `test_gate_condition_is_not_yet_met`. What CAN be said: it refuses a record too thin to illustrate, binds every scene to the evidence, re-inspects each render for reversed impact direction or over-claimed spinal level, parks failures for attorney approval, and hash-verifies locked exhibits.
- **Nothing links into `app.autolaw.ai`** — it has no DNS record. `TRIAL_ENABLED` is false and `LOGIN_URL` is null, and a guard asserts both.
- Every demo panel keeps its "Sample data" mark.

## Feature names, settled by research — do not rename

Ask AL (the app's own name) · Medical Chronologies · Medical Significance Brief · Missing Records & Gaps · QME/AME Deep Analysis (WC) · **Damages Ledger** (the industry converges on "ledger"; "Case Expenses" or "Disbursements" would read as the firm's own outlay) · Demand Packages (PI) · **Case Value & Howell Analysis** (PI) · Negotiation Tracking (PI) · Case Management Summary · Conflicting Records & Opinions · Deadlines & Statute of Limitations · Accident & Injury Illustrations · Discovery Responses (plural — firms say "we have responses due") · Deposition Review.

Two positioning notes from the research: plaintiff firms work on contingency, so "hours saved" lands weakly — sell confidence and "don't blow your one shot" (Filevine's register). And in California workers' comp, deposition prep is billable to the carrier under Labor Code 5710 at roughly $450–500/hour, which nobody in that market is selling.
