Easing

One curve everywhere: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.3, 1). Fast departure, soft arrival. No bounce, no spring, no stagger longer than 60ms. Motion in this product exists to show where a thing came from — a citation card rising out of the timeline row that produced it — never to entertain.

Reduced motion is a parity requirement, not a fallback

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the token durations collapse to 1ms at the :root level, so every transition in the system is suppressed at once and no component needs its own opt-out. Nothing is hidden and no information is conveyed by movement alone — a signal that animates in also carries its state in text and color.
